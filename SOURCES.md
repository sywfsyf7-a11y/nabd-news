# World Pulse — Publisher and image-source policy

This allowlist contains 270 category-and-language assignments covering 168 distinct publisher domains across eight sections. It is a maintained catalog, not a claim that every publisher worldwide is integrated. Google News RSS is used for discovery only; the original publisher's article URL remains the destination for readers.

Arabic-origin stories are displayed only on the Arabic site; English-origin stories only on the English site. Each discovered host must match the configured source allowlist for the story category and language. Some publishers may return no eligible stories because of regional feeds, robots restrictions, or the rights gate.

## Publisher rights gate

BBC (`bbc.com`, `bbc.co.uk`) and Al Jazeera (`aljazeera.net`, `aljazeera.com`) are included in the news discovery catalog in **headline-and-source-link-only mode by default** unless the domain is explicitly approved after rights review. The site does not copy their RSS descriptions or run them through Gemini for a new article body. CNN (`cnn.com`) is included in the regular category-based discovery catalog. Reuters, Associated Press, TechCrunch, ESPN, ScienceDaily and The Guardian remain blocked from reuse of feed excerpts unless specifically approved.

Adding a host to `CONTENT_LICENSE_APPROVED_DOMAINS` is an administrative override, not proof of permission; retain evidence of the licence or written permission and confirm it covers the intended use. A listing, headline, RSS excerpt or attribution does not grant a right to republish a full article or its photograph.

## Image pipeline

1. **Wikimedia Commons** is searched using short keyword phrases derived from each story headline, not by relying on generic stock-photo keys. Results must be relevant and explicitly marked as Public Domain Mark, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. CC BY and CC BY-SA require creator attribution; noncommercial and no-derivatives variants are rejected. The file page and licence URL are kept with the story.
2. **Openverse** is searched by short keyword phrases derived from each headline without a Pexels/Unsplash key. Search results are filtered to CC0, Public Domain Mark, CC BY and CC BY-SA only; non-commercial and no-derivatives licences are excluded. The individual item's image URL, original landing page, creator and licence URL are recorded. Openverse itself cautions that its indexed licence metadata may be inaccurate, so item-level checks and attribution remain necessary.
3. **Library of Congress** photo records are searched without an API key. An individual item must explicitly indicate public domain, no known restrictions, CC0, or free-to-use-and-reuse status; the exact item record is retained. The Library warns that "no known restrictions" is a record-level rights assessment, not a blanket licence, so the item should still be reviewed for its stated restrictions.
4. **NASA Image and Video Library** is queried without an API key only for science, technology, space, and Earth/climate headlines. The image must match the headline metadata; records that explicitly say copyrighted, third-party, restricted, or permission-required are rejected. NASA is credited and its media usage guidance is linked. NASA notes that most of its imagery is generally usable editorially but exceptions can include third-party material, logos, identifiable people, and endorsement implications.
5. Manually reviewed press-kit assets may be used only when the manifest records approval for the intended use and includes the exact source, creator/credit, and licence fields.
6. **Unsplash and Pexels APIs have been removed from the active runtime.** Production photo search does not need either API key.
7. **Gemini-generated article imagery is disabled in production** so AI artwork cannot be confused with documentary reporting.
8. When a photo passes item-level reuse checks, the application validates and optimizes it as a web image and caches the bytes in Supabase (`world_pulse_catalogued_images`). The story record separately retains the original image source URL, creator/credit, landing page and exact licence URL.
9. If no photo passes the checks, the story gets a category-specific, language-aware local SVG editorial illustration, not the fixed A.jpg/E.jpg placeholders. These visuals are labelled as editorial illustration, not documentary photography.
10. Missing-photo and legacy A/E rows are retried after the configured interval (default: 1 hour). Successful cached photos remain durable in Supabase; retry/freshness intervals do not delete article or photo records.

Google News thumbnails, unverified RSS images, publisher-page thumbnails and generic image-search results are never treated as reusable licensed images. An external image is shown only when its item-level source page and licence URL are present in stored provenance. This is a safeguard, not a legal warranty: check the actual licence, attribution, adaptation/commercial limits, trademark, privacy and publicity rights before publication. Publisher text is processed only when the publisher-rights gate permits it; otherwise, only the headline and source link are shown.
